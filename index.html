Data & method

Term premium: FRED series THREEFYTP10, the 10-year term premium from the Kim–Wright three-factor term-structure model (Federal Reserve Board), daily.
10-year yield: FRED DGS10, 10-year Treasury constant-maturity yield (H.15), daily.
Mortgage rate: FRED MORTGAGE30US, Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey 30-year fixed rate, weekly (Thursdays).
Duration: for a par 10-year note with semiannual coupons and yield y, modified duration is computed as D = (1/y)·[1 − (1 + y/2)−20]. Duration falls as yields rise, so the two lines tend to move in opposite directions.
Window math: start and end values are the last observation on or before each date. The price effect is −Dstart × ΔTP, the approximate % change in a 10-year note's price from the term-premium move alone. The weekly correlation uses Thursday-to-Thursday changes in the term premium and the mortgage rate inside the window.
